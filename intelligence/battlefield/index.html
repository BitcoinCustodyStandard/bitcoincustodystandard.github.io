    How to read the battlefield

    Front line
    The current BTC price: the median of the venues’ mid-prices. It moves right when bulls advance and left when bears advance; the camera follows slowly so each advance is visible first.
    Armies
    Bulls stand on resting bids, bears on resting asks. Each price band’s formation grows with its displayed order-book liquidity, so a deep book reads as a deep column. Shading on the ground shows the same depth.
    Champions
    Large walls (a $50 price band holding at least 4× the median band and at least $2M) appear as oversized units in the front rank, labelled with size and price.
    Strikes
    Large aggressive trades arc into the front line from the attacking side: green from buyers lifting offers, red from sellers hitting bids.
    Explosions
    Liquidations. A long liquidation bursts inside the bull ranks (forced selling) with a red shockwave; a short liquidation bursts in the bear ranks (forced buying) with a green one. Size scales with the liquidated notional; $1M+ events shake the field.
    What it is not
    A prediction. Displayed liquidity can be pulled at any time, liquidation feeds cover OKX and Binance only (not the whole market), and the liquidation zones are a model estimate.
